Meet the Leader

Leadership built on
opportunity.

The Rt Hon Ryan Parker is the current Leader of the Liberty Party and Prime Minister of Square Country. He assumed the leadership of the party in September 2025, bringing a renewed focus to economic growth, national security and the country's future.

Under his leadership, Liberty has placed economic opportunity and responsible government at the centre of its political programme. Parker has championed policies intended to encourage private investment, support businesses, strengthen infrastructure and create the conditions for long-term prosperity.

As Prime Minister, Parker has also prioritised national defence, border security and Square Country's international position. His government has pursued a programme of legislative and economic change, including the introduction of the 2026 Think Big Budget.

Parker's leadership is guided by the belief that a strong economy, effective public institutions and individual opportunity are essential to a prosperous and secure Square Country.
