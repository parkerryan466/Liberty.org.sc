A COUNTRY BUILT FOR THE FUTURE

Infrastructure is the foundation of economic opportunity.

A strong economy depends on reliable infrastructure. Roads, electricity networks, water systems and modern transport connections allow businesses to grow, families to thrive and communities to prosper.

Liberty believes Square Country must invest in the infrastructure required for future generations. We will prioritise projects that improve productivity, strengthen regional communities and deliver lasting benefits for taxpayers.

Our Infrastructure Mission

Build a more connected, productive and resilient Square Country through responsible investment, efficient project delivery and stronger partnerships with the private sector.

OUR PRIORITIES

The infrastructure Square Country needs.

Liberty's infrastructure programme will focus on six key areas to improve national productivity, strengthen communities and support long-term economic growth.

01 / TRANSPORT

Better Roads & Transport

Upgrade national highways, improve regional connections and address congestion to make travelling and transporting goods easier and more reliable.

02 / ENERGY

Reliable Energy

Strengthen electricity networks, encourage new generation investment and modernise the national grid to support growing energy demand.

03 / WATER

Water Security

Improve water storage, treatment and distribution infrastructure to support households, agriculture and industry.

04 / REGIONS

Regional Development

Invest in provincial infrastructure that connects regional communities with jobs, markets, services and economic opportunities.

05 / INVESTMENT

Private Sector Partnerships

Encourage responsible private investment and public-private partnerships to expand infrastructure delivery and improve project efficiency.

06 / DELIVERY

Faster Project Delivery

Reduce unnecessary delays, improve planning and strengthen accountability to ensure infrastructure projects deliver value for taxpayers.

A FLAGSHIP INFRASTRUCTURE INITIATIVE

The Northern Prosperity Plan.

The Northern Prosperity Plan is a major commitment to improving infrastructure and unlocking economic opportunities across northern Square Country.

Backed by funding appropriated in the June 2026 Think Big Budget, the plan will support strategic infrastructure investment and strengthen the foundations of regional growth.

  • Improve regional infrastructure and connectivity.
  • Strengthen water security and essential networks.
  • Support businesses, agriculture and industry.
  • Unlock new opportunities for northern communities.
  • Encourage long-term private sector investment.

Unlocking the potential of the North.

Northern Square Country has significant opportunities for economic development. Realising that potential requires reliable infrastructure, better connections and long-term investment.

Liberty will ensure that the funding allocated through the Think Big Budget is directed towards the delivery of the Northern Prosperity Plan and its objectives.

NORTHERN PROSPERITY • 2026
DELIVERING RESULTS

How we will get it done.

Our approach is focused on practical investment, responsible spending and getting important projects completed.

Modern Transport Networks

A modern transport network is essential to a productive economy. Liberty will focus on improving the quality, capacity and reliability of national transport infrastructure.

  • Upgrade important national highways.
  • Improve freight and logistics corridors.
  • Address major urban traffic bottlenecks.
  • Improve regional road connections.
  • Prioritise safety improvements.

Energy for Economic Growth

Businesses and households need dependable electricity. We will support a stronger energy system capable of meeting the demands of a growing economy.

  • Encourage investment in electricity generation.
  • Modernise national transmission infrastructure.
  • Improve grid resilience and reliability.
  • Support private sector energy investment.
  • Plan for future electricity demand.

Water Infrastructure & Security

Reliable water infrastructure supports communities, agriculture and industry. We will encourage sustainable investment in essential water networks.

  • Improve water storage capacity.
  • Modernise treatment and distribution systems.
  • Upgrade ageing infrastructure.
  • Support regional water security.
  • Maintain environmental safeguards.

Regional Infrastructure Investment

Every part of Square Country should have the opportunity to benefit from economic growth. Regional infrastructure will be an important part of our national development plan.

  • Support high-value regional projects.
  • Improve access to employment and services.
  • Strengthen connections to regional industries.
  • Work with local authorities and businesses.
  • Support sustainable regional development.

Private Investment & Partnerships

Government cannot and should not be the only source of infrastructure investment. Liberty will encourage private sector participation where it delivers value.

  • Encourage competitive infrastructure investment.
  • Explore suitable public-private partnerships.
  • Reduce unnecessary investment barriers.
  • Improve procurement processes.
  • Maintain appropriate public accountability.

Responsible Project Management

Infrastructure investment must be carefully planned, transparently managed and delivered efficiently. Taxpayers deserve confidence that public money is being spent responsibly.

  • Require clear project business cases.
  • Improve cost-benefit assessments.
  • Monitor project timelines and expenditure.
  • Publish progress updates on major projects.
  • Address unnecessary delays and cost overruns.
RESPONSIBLE INVESTMENT

Building responsibly. Investing for the long term.

Liberty believes that infrastructure investment must be ambitious, but it must also be financially responsible. Government should prioritise projects that deliver measurable economic benefits and improve the lives of Square Country's people.

We will make effective use of existing appropriations, including funding provided through the June 2026 Think Big Budget for the Northern Prosperity Plan. We will also encourage private investment where it can deliver better outcomes and value for taxpayers.

Major projects must be supported by sound planning, transparent procurement and appropriate financial oversight. We will focus on delivering infrastructure that Square Country can afford to build, operate and maintain.
